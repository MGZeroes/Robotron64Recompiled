Texture pack for Robotron 64: Recompiled, loaded from this folder as a mod.

Put replacement images (PNG or DDS) in this folder and assign them in RT64's inspector (F1, needs Dev Mode),
then press "Save directory" there to update rt64.json. Toggle the pack off and on in the Mods menu to reload.

How it all works: docs/TEXTURE_PACKS.md in the Robotron 64: Recompiled repository.
Images made from the game's textures are derived from its art: share them only as a separate pack.
